About Me

I'm Elliot — an instructor, artist, and live events technician based in Ipswich. I've spent the last six years at the Academy of Live Technology (ALT) at Production Park: first as a student completing both my BA (Hons) and MA there, now as a member of staff. That trajectory shapes how I teach — I know exactly what it's like to sit in those seats.

My specialist area is video for live performance: LED walls, projection mapping, and content creation for live events. I lead the Staging module and contribute across all year groups and courses, up to Masters level. I'm currently working towards a PGCert in Higher Education, developing the academic side of a career that's always been hands-on first.

Outside the day job I run Ritual Syntax — a generative and digital abstract art practice. The work is rooted in systems, noise, and controlled chaos: code-driven visuals that sit somewhere between occult aesthetics and technical process. This site is part of that practice.

I also build tools — colour utilities, generative studios, scheduling systems — wherever a problem exists that a bit of code can solve more elegantly than anything else available.
